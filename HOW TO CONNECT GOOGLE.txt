CONNECTING GOOGLE CALENDAR AND CLASSROOM
========================================

What this does: the tutor on the laptop reads your Google Calendar and your
Google Classroom four times a day, and whenever you press Sync now. It
shows up on the School tab of the web app, and Classroom homework counts on
the Subjects tab. Nothing is added to your list unless you press "Add to my
list".

Classroom is read only: it can't change or hand in anything.

Calendar: it can also WRITE a date for you (a test, a homework due date),
but only when you ask, and only after it has shown you the exact words and
you've said yes. It can change or take off dates it wrote itself. It never
changes or deletes anything else on your calendar. See PART 5.

About 10 minutes. Do the parts in order.


PART 1: SWITCH THE TWO GOOGLE SERVICES ON
-----------------------------------------
1.  In Chrome, paste this into the address bar and press Enter:
      https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/calendar-json.googleapis.com?project=tutor-anaya
    If it asks you to pick an account, pick the SAME one you used for
    Firebase. If it asks you to agree to terms, tick the box, click
    "Agree and continue".
2.  Click the blue "Enable" button. Wait until it says "API Enabled".
3.  Paste this into the address bar and press Enter:
      https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/classroom.googleapis.com?project=tutor-anaya
4.  Click the blue "Enable" button. Wait until it says "API Enabled".


PART 2: THE PERMISSION SCREEN
-----------------------------
5.  Paste this into the address bar and press Enter:
      https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/overview?project=tutor-anaya
6.  Click "Get started".
7.  App name: type  Tutor
    User support email: pick your email from the list. Click "Next".
8.  Audience: pick "External". Click "Next".
9.  Contact information: type your email. Click "Next".
10. Tick "I agree". Click "Continue", then "Create".
11. On the left, click "Audience".
12. Under "Publishing status" click "Publish app", then "Confirm".
    (This matters. Without it Google makes you connect again every week.)


PART 3: THE KEY FOR THE LAPTOP
------------------------------
13. On the left, click "Clients".
14. Click "Create client".
15. Application type: pick "Desktop app".
16. Name: type  tutor-laptop   Click "Create".
17. A box shows "Client ID" and "Client secret". Leave it open.
18. Open the folder with main.py in it. Right click  firebase_config.json
    and open it with Notepad.
19. Put a comma at the very end of the "password" line if there isn't one.
20. Under that line, add these two lines. Copy the Client ID between the
    first pair of quote marks and the Client secret between the second:

      "googleClientId": "",
      "googleClientSecret": ""

    The LAST line before the closing } must NOT end in a comma.
    It should end up looking like this (your own values in the gaps):

      {
        "apiKey": "...",
        "projectId": "tutor-anaya",
        "email": "...",
        "password": "",
        "googleClientId": "1234-abcd.apps.googleusercontent.com",
        "googleClientSecret": "GOCSPX-..."
      }

21. Press Ctrl+S. Close Notepad.
22. Right click the cat, click "Restart the tutor".


PART 4: SAY YES
---------------
23. Right click the cat.
24. Click "Connect Google". Chrome opens.
    (If nothing opens, click the Log tab in the tutor. It says why.)
25. Pick the account your Classroom is on (your school one).
26. It says "Google hasn't verified this app". That's because it's your
    own app. Click "Advanced", then "Go to Tutor (unsafe)".
27. Tick every box it shows. Click "Continue".
28. The tab says "Google is connected. You can close this tab." Close it.
29. Open the web app, click "School". Within a minute it says
    "Last checked" with the time.


PART 5: LETTING IT WRITE DATES ON YOUR CALENDAR
-----------------------------------------------
You only do this once. If you connected Google before 3 October's update,
the tutor can read your calendar but not write on it yet.

30. Right click the cat.
31. Click "Connect Google". Chrome opens.
32. Pick the same account as before.
33. "Google hasn't verified this app": click "Advanced", then
    "Go to Tutor (unsafe)".
34. There is now one more box, about your calendar events
    ("View and edit events on all your calendars"). Tick EVERY box,
    that one too. Click "Continue".
35. The tab says "Google is connected. You can close this tab." Close it.
36. Wait one minute. Open the web app, click "School". Under
    "Put a date on Google Calendar" there is now a box to type in.

How to use it, on the web app:
    Type what it is, pick the day, click "Show me what it will write".
    Read the words. Click "Write it", or "Don't".
How to use it, in the tutor's chat on the laptop:
    Type:  put physics test on my calendar 12 oct
    It says exactly what it will write. Type  yes  or  no.
    To change one it wrote:  move physics test on my calendar to 14 oct
    To remove one it wrote:  take physics test off my calendar
A time is optional:  put french oral on my calendar 14 oct at 9:30

You did NOT need to change anything in Google Cloud for this.


PART 6: WHEN SCHOOL IT SAYS YES (SWITCHING TO YOUR SCHOOL ACCOUNT)
------------------------------------------------------------------
What to send IT, so they can allow the app. They need the Client ID
(NOT the secret. Never send anyone the secret.):

    a. Open the folder with main.py in it. Right click
       firebase_config.json, open it with Notepad.
    b. Copy what's between the quote marks after "googleClientId".
       It ends in  .apps.googleusercontent.com
    c. Close Notepad WITHOUT saving.
    d. Send IT that, and tell them: "Please mark this OAuth Client ID as
       Trusted in the Google Admin console, under Security, API controls,
       App access control. It needs Calendar and Classroom."
       The privacy page for them to read is:
       https://anaya-tutor.netlify.app/privacy.html

When they say it's done:

37. Right click the cat.
38. Click "Connect Google". Chrome opens. You do NOT need to press
    Disconnect first.
39. It shows a list of accounts. Click your SCHOOL one. If it isn't in
    the list, click "Use another account" and sign in with the school
    email.
40. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app": "Advanced", then
    "Go to Tutor (unsafe)". (Once IT has trusted it, this may not show.)
41. Tick every box. Click "Continue".
42. The tab says "Google is connected. You can close this tab." Close it.
43. Wait one minute. On the web app's School tab the "Calendar:" line
    now shows your school email, and Classroom fills in.

Good to know:
    The tutor is connected to ONE Google account at a time. Connecting
    the school one replaces your own one. To go back, press Connect
    Google again and pick the other account.
    Dates the tutor wrote on your OWN calendar stay there. While the
    school account is connected it leaves those alone, because they
    belong to the other account.
    If the School tab ever says "Google cancelled the connection", just
    press Connect Google again. Nothing is lost.


IF IT GOES WRONG
----------------
"Something went wrong" or "Access blocked" when you tick the calendar box
with the SCHOOL account:
    School allows reading but not writing. Press Connect Google again
    and leave the calendar events box unticked. Reading still works; the
    tutor will say it can't write.

The tutor says "I can only read your calendar at the moment":
    Do PART 5. The calendar events box wasn't ticked.

"That didn't go on your Google Calendar: ... Calendar API has not been
used" or "403":
    Do steps 1 and 2 again (the Calendar "Enable" button).

"Access blocked" or "admin policy" in step 25 to 27:
    Your school doesn't let outside apps read Classroom. Nothing you can
    do from your side. Do steps 23 to 28 again with your own Gmail
    instead: you still get your personal calendar.

The School tab says "Problem: Classroom: 403 ...":
    Same thing: the school blocks Classroom. The calendar still works.

"Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch":
    In step 15 something other than "Desktop app" was picked. Do steps 13
    to 22 again and pick "Desktop app".

The tutor's Log tab says the sync port isn't open:
    Restart the tutor and press Connect Google again.

To switch it off: right click the tutor's tray icon, "Disconnect Google".
