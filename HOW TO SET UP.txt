TUTOR WEB APP: HOW TO SET IT UP
================================

Do these in order. Each step is one click or one thing to type.
Nothing leaves your laptop until PART 3 is finished.

You need: a Google account, and about 15 minutes.


PART 1: MAKE THE FIREBASE PROJECT (the place the data lives)
------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Open Chrome. Go to  https://console.firebase.google.com
2.  Sign in with your Google account if it asks.
3.  Click the big box that says "Create a project" (or "Add project").
4.  Type a name:  tutor-anaya   then click "Continue".
5.  It asks about Google Analytics. Click the switch so it is OFF.
    Click "Create project". Wait. Click "Continue".

Turn on sign in:
6.  On the left, click "Build", then click "Authentication".
7.  Click "Get started".
8.  Click "Email/Password".
9.  Click the FIRST switch so it is on. Click "Save".
10. At the top, click the "Users" tab. Click "Add user".
11. Type an email (it can be your real one) and a NEW password that you
    don't use anywhere else. Write the password down. Click "Add user".

Turn on the database:
12. On the left, click "Build", then click "Firestore Database".
13. Click "Create database".
14. Location: pick any, click "Next".
15. Pick "Start in production mode". Click "Create" (or "Enable").
16. At the top, click the "Rules" tab.
17. Delete everything in the box.
18. Open the file  firestore.rules  (it is in this folder) in Notepad.
    Select all of it, copy it, and paste it into the box in Chrome.
19. Click "Publish".

Get the keys:
20. Top left, click the little cog, then click "Project settings".
21. Scroll down to "Your apps". Click the icon that looks like  </>
22. App nickname: type  tutor   Do NOT tick Firebase Hosting.
    Click "Register app".
23. You now see a block with apiKey, authDomain, projectId and appId.
    Leave this page open. You need it for PART 2 and PART 3.


PART 2: TELL THE WEB APP ABOUT IT
---------------------------------
24. In this folder, right click  firebase-config.js  and open it with
    Notepad.
25. Replace each PASTE_..._HERE with the matching value from the page in
    step 23. Keep the quote marks. Where it says PASTE_PROJECT_ID_HERE
    twice, put the projectId both times.
26. Press Ctrl+S to save. Close Notepad.


PART 3: TELL THE DESKTOP TUTOR ABOUT IT
---------------------------------------
27. Go up one folder, to the folder that has  main.py  in it.
28. Right click  firebase_config.json  and open it with Notepad.
29. Put the apiKey between the quotes after "apiKey".
30. Put the projectId between the quotes after "projectId".
31. Put the email from step 11 between the quotes after "email".
32. Put the password from step 11 between the quotes after "password".
33. Press Ctrl+S to save. Close Notepad.
34. Right click the cat in the tray (bottom right of the screen) and
    click "Restart the tutor".
35. Open the tutor and look at the Activity tab. It should say
    "Cloud sync is on."
    If it says "Cloud sync isn't getting through", read the reason it
    gives. Wrong password and a typo in the apiKey are the usual two.
36. Once it has said "Cloud sync is on" and a minute has gone by, you can
    open  firebase_config.json  again and delete the password (leave the
    two quote marks). The tutor keeps its own pass after the first time.


PART 4: PUT THE WEB APP ONLINE
------------------------------
The same way the Infinity Bites site went up.
37. Go to  https://app.netlify.com/drop
38. Drag this whole folder (tutor-pwa) onto the page. Wait for it to
    finish. It gives you a web address ending in .netlify.app. Copy it.
39. Back in the Firebase page: left side, "Build", "Authentication",
    then the "Settings" tab, then "Authorized domains".
    Click "Add domain". Paste the netlify address WITHOUT the https://
    at the front. Click "Add".
40. Open the netlify address. Sign in with the email and password from
    step 11. You should see Today, Subjects and YouTube.

On your phone:
41. Open the same address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
42. Android: tap the three dots, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install".
    iPhone: tap the share square, then "Add to Home Screen".


PUTTING A NEW VERSION OF THE WEB APP ONLINE
-------------------------------------------
Do this every time the files in this folder change.
1.  Go to  https://app.netlify.com   and sign in.
2.  Click your tutor site in the list (the one ending .netlify.app).
3.  At the top, click "Deploys".
4.  Scroll to the bottom. There is a box that says
    "Drag and drop your project output folder here".
5.  Open File Explorer, find the  tutor-pwa  folder, and drag the WHOLE
    folder onto that box. Wait until it says "Published".
6.  On the laptop: open the web app and press Ctrl+Shift+R once.
    On the phone: close the app fully and open it again. If it still
    looks old, do it a second time.
Then restart the tutor: right click the cat, click "Restart the tutor".


WHAT THE WEB APP DOES NOW
-------------------------
Sync now (top of the page)
    Asks the laptop to send everything now. It says "Synced" with the
    time when the laptop has answered. If it says the laptop isn't
    answering, the tutor is closed or the laptop is asleep.
    On the laptop the same thing is: right click the cat's icon in the
    tray, click "Sync with the web app now".

The YouTube question
    After every 20 minutes of YouTube in a day, a box at the top asks if
    it was worth it. One press answers it. The note is optional.
    Your answers show on the YouTube tab.

Chats tab
    1. In Gemini, open the revision chat.
    2. Click anywhere in the chat, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
    3. In the web app, click "Chats", then "Add a Gemini chat".
    4. Click in the big box and press Ctrl+V.
    5. Pick the subject, or leave "Work it out for me".
    6. Click "Save it".

Papers tab
    Shows the PDFs, pictures and text files in your REVISION folder on the
    Desktop, by subject. They come up from the laptop a few at a time, so
    a big folder takes a while the first day. A file that says "Still
    coming up from the laptop" isn't ready yet. Files over 15 MB stay on
    the laptop only.
    If your REVISION folder is somewhere other than the Desktop: open
    firebase_config.json next to main.py in Notepad and add this line
    after the "password" line (put a comma at the end of the password
    line first), with your own folder, and two backslashes wherever the
    path has one:
        "revisionFolder": "C:\\Users\\anaya\\Documents\\REVISION"


TO TURN THE CLOUD OFF
---------------------
Delete  firebase_config.json  next to main.py (or put PASTE back in the
apiKey) and restart the tutor. Everything stays on the laptop as before.
